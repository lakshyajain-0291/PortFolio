# Design System: Lakshya Jain Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Measured Record"**

The portfolio has two visual worlds. They share one typed content model (`shared/content/`), one diagram layout engine and flow player (`shared/content/diagram/`) and one 3D scene kit (`shared/three/scenes/`), and they share no visual tokens. The **Systems Paper** (`templates/paper/`) is the site's default edition: `/` with no parameter, or `?template=paper` (default set in `src/config/env.ts`). It is a portfolio typeset as a short systems paper. It has a white page, near-black serif ink, one citation blue, hairline rules, numbered sections, numbered figures and tables, and a margin column. The person comes first and the work is evidence: the home page reads as a portfolio, and each system opens into its own case-study page (`?project=<slug>`). The **Engineering Atlas** (`?template=atlas`, `templates/atlas/`) is a drawing set. It uses a Prussian-blue ground with white hairline linework, a faint construction grid, framed sheets with zone ticks, condensed Archivo caps, and a single safety-orange revision colour. Both treat each number as evidence. A metric always carries its kind (measured, count, target, projected, self-reported) and its source, and the same architecture spec drives each system's drawing, figure and 3D model.

The worlds are flat, square-cornered and line-built. Depth comes from rules and frames, never from surfaces lifted off the page. Colour is rationed. Each world has one accent, and it marks selection, focus, the playing step and what matters most. Both worlds avoid the hero-plus-cards developer portfolio. The Paper also avoids the project-first portfolio and the cream-and-terracotta editorial default, and the Atlas avoids neon dark-tech and glowing particles. Motion in the Paper is explanatory, not decorative: figures walk through a system's flows step by step, and every walkthrough can be paused, jumped and read as text.

This file does not govern the legacy editions. Templates 0 and 1 (`src/`, self-hosted fonts in `public/fonts/t1`), template 2 (`src2/`, Fraunces, Schibsted Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono in `public/fonts/editorial`) and template 3 (`src3/`, the terminal) were refreshed independently on 2026-10-13. They keep their own Tailwind and shadcn styling. The Paper's colophon links to them as other editions, but do not import their tokens into the Paper or the Atlas, and do not use this file to restyle them.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Each world is scoped to its own root class (`html.paper`, `html.atlas`) with its own custom properties (`--p-*`, `--a-*`).
- Square corners everywhere, with no radius tokens beyond zero.
- Structure is built from 1px rules and frames, not from cards or shadows.
- Each world has exactly one accent hue, used for selection, focus, lit and playing state.
- Every number is set in tabular figures and paired with its kind and source.
- Diagrams are SVG and stay readable without animation. Walkthroughs never autoplay under reduced motion, and 3D is always an opt-in, lazy-loaded view of the same system.
- Self-hosted variable fonts under OFL (`public/fonts/`), with no CDN font loading.

## Colors

The Paper uses a two-mode ink-on-page palette plus one citation blue. The Atlas uses a two-ground drawing palette, blueprint and whiteprint, plus one revision orange.

### Primary
- **Citation Blue** (`paper-citation-blue`; dark `paper-night-citation-blue`): the Paper's only accent. It colours links (with a 45% underline that goes solid on hover), citation markers, the focus outline, the active Contents entry, the "N more" toggles, lit diagram nodes and edges, the travelling pulse, the filling timeline segment, the pressed flow-picker underline, the active scroll-story step marker, lit 3D scene labels, and the selected part row (as `paper-citation-fill`). Text selection uses `paper-select`.
- **Revision Orange** (`atlas-revision-orange`; whiteprint `atlas-whiteprint-revision`): the Atlas's only accent. It marks the selected component's tick, lit connections and arrowheads, the selected part row (as `atlas-revision-fill`), the focus outline, link-hover underlines and the text-selection wash. It is never a fill for large areas, never a text colour for prose, and never decoration.

### Neutral
- **Paper Page and Ink, three steps** (`paper-page`, `paper-ink`, `-2`, `-3`): the page and the text levels. Ink 2 is for bylines, captions, margin facts and the playing step's text. Ink 3 is for numbers in headings, list markers, field labels, evidence kinds in italic, completed timeline segments and steps the scroll story has not reached.
- **Paper Rules** (`paper-rule`, `paper-rule-strong`): row dividers and unplayed timeline segments, and frame rules (figure, 3D model, view switch, play button, citation preview), respectively.
- **Paper Fill** (`paper-fill`; dark `paper-night-fill`): loading skeletons, the pressed Drawing / 3D model option, and the faint fill of 3D boxes. It is never a card background.
- **Paper Night set** (`paper-night-*`): dark mode. It follows `prefers-color-scheme` unless the reader picks a mode with the footer segment. The 3D model reads the same choice and switches between the `paper` and `paper-dark` scene palettes.
- **Prussian Ground** (`atlas-prussian-ground`): the blueprint drawing ground behind every sheet. **Prussian Raised** (`atlas-prussian-ground-raised`) is used only as the fill of a lit diagram node.
- **Sheet White, three steps** (`atlas-sheet-white`, `-2`, `-3`): Atlas text in three levels. Step 1 is titles and values, step 2 is prose and notes, and step 3 is zone ticks, row marks and field labels. All three pass AA on the ground.
- **Hairlines, three alphas** (`atlas-hairline`, `-2`, `-3`): the frame, the inner divisions and the faint row rules. Line hierarchy comes from alpha, not from width.
- **Construction Grid** (`atlas-construction-grid`, `-major`): the 24px and 120px grid drawn on the body background. Never use it as a border colour.
- **Whiteprint set** (`atlas-whiteprint-*`): the light variant, set with `[data-theme='white']`. It keeps the same roles as blueprint, with blue linework on a cool white ground.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Each world has exactly one accent hue, and it means selected, focused, playing or matters most. A second accent colour is a defect.

**The Lit-and-Dim Rule.** When something is selected or a flow step is shown, the related parts light up in the accent and everything else dims (Paper opacity 0.28, Atlas 0.32). The same state drives the diagram, the 3D model and the text, because all of them read it.

**The Print Truth Rule.** Both worlds ship a print stylesheet that switches to black or blue ink on white, drops navigation chrome, players and view switches, unpins the scroll story and prints link URLs. A colour that only works on screen is not part of the system.

## Typography

**Paper Body Font:** Source Serif 4 variable with optical sizes (opsz 8–60), roman and italic, with Iowan Old Style and Georgia as fallbacks
**Paper Mono Font:** Source Code Pro, with ui-monospace as a fallback
**Atlas Display/Body Font:** Archivo variable (wdth 62–125, wght 100–900), with Helvetica Neue and Arial as fallbacks
**Atlas Mono Font:** Martian Mono variable (wdth 75–112.5), with ui-monospace as a fallback

**Character:** The Paper is all serif. It sets headings on large optical sizes, uses italics for availability and for weak evidence kinds, uses small caps for labels inside the 3D model, and uses mono only for numbers and identifiers. The Atlas uses one family at different widths. Uppercase Archivo condensed to 62–80% sets titles and labels like lettering on a drawing, and normal-width Archivo sets the prose.

### Hierarchy
- **Paper Display** (600, opsz 60, `text-wrap: balance`): the name as the paper title on the home page, and the system's name as the title of its case-study page.
- **Paper Headline** (600, opsz 36): numbered section heads, on the home page (1 Experience to 7 Code record) and on case studies (1 Problem, 2 How it works, 3 The system, 4 Evidence). The number is set in mono at 0.8em in ink 3. References carries no number.
- **Paper Work Title** (600, opsz 32): the name of each selected-work entry, in ink, with its link underline hidden until hover.
- **Paper Title** (600, opsz 28): third-level heads. Experience and project entry heads are 1.1875rem at 600. Flow names and the colophon head are 1.0625rem at 650.
- **Paper Body** (400, 1.125rem/1.62, measure 41rem): all prose. Run-in heads ("About.") are 650. The work summary, the author line and the lede are 1.1875rem; the case-study lede is 1.3125rem in ink 2.
- **Paper Caption** (0.9375rem, ink 2): figure and table captions with a bold "Figure n." or "Table n." prefix, the playing step's text, the flow picker, the Contents, the margin facts and the reference list.
- **Paper Label** (0.8125rem, ink 3): field names in the at-a-glance list and the case-study meta list (Most recent, Role, Stack).
- **Paper Scene Label** (15px, all small caps, 0.04em tracking): component names floating in the 3D model, on an 82% page wash.
- **Paper Mono** (0.86em, tabular): numbers, metric values, citation markers, step counters ("2/6"), tags and evidence kinds.
- **Atlas Display** (800, wdth 62%, uppercase): the name in the title block only. On phones it drops to `clamp(2.6rem, 15vw, 3.75rem)`.
- **Atlas Headline** (700, wdth 66%, uppercase): sheet titles in the header strip. Register entries use a smaller sibling (650, `clamp(1.35rem, 2.1vw, 1.95rem)`). Detail-sheet titles use 700 at wdth 66% with `clamp(1.4rem, 2.4vw, 1.9rem)`.
- **Atlas Dimension value** (700, wdth 66%, `clamp(1.9rem, 3.4vw, 2.6rem)`, tabular lining figures): measured numbers set above a dimension line.
- **Atlas Title** (700, wdth 72%, uppercase): schedule row heads such as experience and other projects.
- **Atlas Body** (400, 1rem/1.55): notes and prose. The notes lede is 500 at `clamp(1.15rem, 1.8vw, 1.35rem)` with a 1.35 line height.
- **Atlas Label** (600–650, wdth 75–80%, uppercase, 0.05–0.06em tracking): block headings inside a sheet, title-block field names (Role, School, Status), table heads, diagram node labels and kind tags.
- **Atlas Mono** (wdth 87.5%, 0.75rem, tabular): sheet numbers, zone ticks, connection numbers, note counters, numeric table cells and on-drawing dimension text.

### Named Rules
**The Mono-Is-Data Rule.** Monospace is reserved for data such as coordinates, sheet numbers, identifiers, step counters, numeric cells and evidence kinds. Prose, headings and buttons are never set in mono.

**The Optical-Size Rule (Paper).** Each Paper heading tier sets `opsz` to match its size (60, 36, 32 or 28). Body text uses `font-optical-sizing: auto`.

**The Width-Not-Weight Rule (Atlas).** Hierarchy in the Atlas comes mostly from the width axis plus uppercase. Do not reach for a new family or an outline style to make a heading louder.

## Layout

**Paper.** A single 41rem measure sits on a page of at most 76rem. A sticky running head spans the page: on the home page it names the current numbered section, on a case study it names the person and the system, and on the right it always offers Resume and Email. At 70rem and wider a 14rem right margin column opens beside the title block, separated by a `clamp(3rem, 6vw, 6rem)` gap and a 1px left rule. It holds the "at a glance" facts (most recent role, education, top recognition), divided by hairlines, and then the Contents, which is the home page's navigation. Below 70rem the facts become an auto-fit grid of 13rem cells and the Contents sits inline after them.

The home page is portfolio-first and reads in this order: title block (name, author line, availability, action line), the first-person About under a hairline, the margin, then the numbered sections Experience, Selected work, Other projects, Recognition and leadership, Skills, Education and Code record, then the unnumbered References and the Colophon. Empty sections drop out and the numbers close up. Selected work is a stack of short entries divided by hairlines (`paper-work-pad`), each with title, byline, one-sentence summary, two headline metrics with their kind, a looping figure and a "Read the case study" link. Case studies live on their own pages, routed with the History API (`templates/paper/route.ts`) so back, refresh and shared links land on the same page; Back returns the reader to the entry they opened.

Figures, the scroll story, parts lists and evidence tables span the full page width; text stays in the measure. Sections are spaced `paper-section-gap` apart. At 64rem and wider the case-study figure becomes a pinned scroll story: the figure sticks under the running head (top 2.7rem) and each flow step below it holds at least 22vh, so one step at a time crosses the reading line. Below 64rem the same figure falls back to the looping player. Tables stack into label-prefixed rows below 40rem. Breakpoints are 70rem, 64rem and 40rem.

**Atlas.** The page is a stack of sheets inside a centred set (max 92rem, padded `clamp(0.75rem, 2.5vw, 2rem)`, sheet gap `clamp(1.5rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`). Every sheet uses a 3×3 grid. A zone band (1.25rem, 1rem below 860px) runs on each side and is divided into tick cells, and the body sits in the middle cell. A header strip divides the sheet into number, title and meta cells. The cover is a two-column grid (1.3fr / 1fr). The drawing register runs down the left and stretches to the full sheet height on desktop. Cover notes sit at the top right, and the title block sits bottom-right. Below 860px the title block moves to the top, the register comes second and the notes come last. System sheets put the drawing beside a sticky notes column (`minmax(20rem, 27rem)`) and stack below 1100px. A sticky running bar carries the name, the current sheet and the actions. On phones, an active flow stepper sticks to the bottom of the viewport so it travels with the drawing. Breakpoints are 1100px, 860px, 640px and 520px.

**The Person-First Rule (Paper).** The home page answers who, what they want and where they have worked before it shows any system. Projects are evidence, set as short entries; their depth lives on their own pages.

**The Stretch-to-Sheet Rule.** Atlas regions fill their frame cell. Lists such as the register share the available height rather than floating in empty space, and spacing between regions is a rule, not a gap.

## Elevation & Depth

Both worlds are flat. They do not use drop shadows for elevation. The Paper builds depth only from rules: double-weight 1.5px ink rules above and below tables, strong rules above and below figures and the 3D model, hairlines between work entries and margin facts, and a hairline beside the margin column. The Atlas builds depth from its line hierarchy (frame, inner division, row rule) and from the construction grid behind the sheets. Sheets sit on an 82% ground wash, so the grid reads faintly through them.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pinned figure rule** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 var(--p-rule)`): the bottom hairline of the Paper's pinned scroll-story figure, which overlaps the steps scrolling beneath it. It is a rule, not a blur.
- **Lit scene label, Paper** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 var(--p-accent)`): a 3D scene label that belongs to the current step.
- **Inset selection bar** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -2px 0 var(--a-ink)`): pressed Atlas toggles and the hovered register title. It works as an underline, not as elevation.
- **Primary hover bar** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -3px 0 var(--a-line-2)`): hover on the Atlas primary button.
- **Lit scene label, Atlas** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -2px 0 var(--a-rev)`): a 3D scene label that belongs to the current step.
- **Docked stepper** (`box-shadow: 0 -10px 18px -12px rgba(4, 16, 30, 0.6)`): only for the Atlas flow stepper while it is sticky at the bottom on phones, to separate it from the drawing that scrolls beneath.

### Named Rules
**The Rule-Not-Shadow Rule.** Separate regions with a 1px rule at the right step. A shadow is allowed only for a layer that genuinely overlaps scrolling content, such as the pinned figure or the docked stepper, or as an inset state bar.

## Shapes

All corners are square (radius 0) in both worlds, including buttons, segments, the play button, the view switch, timeline segments, tags, popovers and diagram boxes. The Paper focus outline has a 1px radius, which is the only exception. Form language comes from drafting. Diagram strokes are 1–1.25px at rest and 1.6–2px when lit. External components use dashed outlines (Paper 5 3, Atlas 6 4) and planned components use dotted ones (2 3 / 2 4). Groups use a round-dot dash in the Paper (1 4) and a chain-dash in the Atlas (10 3 2 3). Async edges are dashed at 5 4. Paper connection markers are 20px squares; the travelling pulse is the one round form, a 3.5px dot. The Paper's "N more" disclosure uses an authored chevron (two 1.5px ink-3 borders, rotated −45° closed and 45° open), not a glyph. The play and pause marks are inline SVG on a 12-unit grid. The Atlas draws dimension lines with extension ticks and 45° arrow tips, and its code-record bars are hatched at 135°; its icons come from one authored set on a 16px grid with a 1.5 stroke and square caps (`templates/atlas/components/Icon.tsx`). The 3D models are orthographic, wireframe-only and unlit, and they use the scene palettes in `shared/three/scenes/kit.tsx`: `blue` and `white` mirror the Atlas sheets, and `paper` and `paper-dark` mirror the Paper page (ground, ink line, a dimmed line, the citation blue as the lit colour, and the page fill). The orthographic zoom scales down with canvas width (below 880px) so the whole model stays in frame.

## Components

### Links and Actions (Paper)
- **Links:** citation-blue text with a 1px underline at 45% that goes solid on hover (160ms ease-out). In print, external URLs are appended in mono.
- **Actions line:** Resume (PDF), Email, GitHub and LinkedIn appear as plain links in a wrapped row. The Resume link is set at weight 650 to mark it as primary. "Read the case study" on each work entry is the same 650 link.
- **Paper buttons** exist only where a control has state: selectable parts, the play button, the flow picker, timeline segments, the view switch and the theme segment.

### Buttons (Atlas)
- **Shape:** square, at least 2.5rem tall (small variant 2rem), with a 1px border at hairline step 2.
- **Default:** transparent, sheet-white text, 0.875rem at weight 600, and an optional 16px icon before the text.
- **Primary:** inverted, with a sheet-white fill and ground-coloured text. Each surface has one primary action, and on the title block that action is the Resume.
- **Hover / Focus:** the border goes to full hairline and a 10% wash fills the button (160ms ease-out). Primary keeps its fill and gains a 3px inset bottom bar. Focus is a 2px revision-orange outline at a 3px offset.
- **Pressed toggle:** a wash plus a 2px inset ink bar. **Segmented control:** buttons share one outer border and are divided by internal hairlines.

### Cards / Containers
There are no cards. The Paper container is the **section**, which has a numbered head with no frame; inside it, selected work is a run of **entries** divided by hairlines. The Atlas container is the **sheet**, which has a frame, zone ticks and a header strip.

### At a Glance and Contents (Paper navigation)
A definition list of three facts (label in Paper Label, value in ink, a sub-line in ink 2), then a numbered Contents list whose current entry turns citation blue as the reader scrolls. The running head repeats the current section with its number.

### Work Entry (Paper)
Title in Paper Work Title, a byline (role · context · period), a one-sentence summary, two headline metrics (mono value, serif label, italic kind in parentheses; measured and counted numbers are chosen first), the system's looping figure, and links. The whole entry is the home page's only project unit.

### Looping Figure (signature, Paper)
The home-page figure walks through one flow of the system on a loop, one step every 3.8s (`templates/paper/components/Diagram.tsx`, timing from `shared/content/diagram/flowPlayer.ts`).
- **Controls:** a square 2.25rem play/pause button with a strong-rule border (border goes to ink on hover); a flow picker of plain text buttons whose pressed option carries a 1px citation-blue underline; a segmented timeline with one 3px segment per step (rule when unplayed, ink 3 when done, the current one filling in citation blue over the step's time), each segment a jump target; and a line with the mono step counter, the step title in ink and its text in ink 2.
- **Figure state:** the step's components and connections light, everything else dims, and a citation-blue pulse travels along each lit edge (1.6s). While a step is shown, lit connection markers carry the step number and unlit markers are hidden, so the figure never shows a number the counter doesn't.
- **Behaviour:** it holds while the pointer or focus is inside, pauses when less than 35% is on screen or the tab is hidden, and never autoplays under reduced motion (the reader can still press Play or jump). The step text announces politely only when not running.

### Story Figure (signature, Paper)
On case studies at 64rem and wider, "The system" is a pinned scroll story. The figure sticks under the running head with a hairline beneath it; the flows' steps scroll past in ink 3, and the step crossing the reading line turns ink with a citation-blue list marker, lights the figure and becomes its caption ("Schedule a task, 1/6 Submit."). Selecting a component takes over until the next step scrolls in. Below 64rem the same figure is a Looping Figure. The Parts list follows as the figure's accessible text equivalent.

### Drawing / 3D Model Switch (Paper)
A two-option segmented control (square, strong-rule outer border, internal hairline, 2.25rem tall); the pressed option fills with Paper Fill. The 3D model loads lazily in its own chunk only when chosen, sits between strong rules at `clamp(20rem, 46vw, 30rem)` (in the story, `clamp(18rem, 42vh, 26rem)`), follows the current step, and carries a quiet "Drag to turn the model." hint. If WebGL is unavailable or the scene fails, a sentence says so and points back to the drawing.

### Parts List and Flow Stepper
- **Part row:** a full-width button at least 2.75rem tall. In the Paper it has a 1px left rule that turns citation blue when the row is pressed. In the Atlas it has a mono mark and a hairline beneath. The pressed row gets the accent fill.
- **Connections list (Paper):** numbered rows on hairlines; rows of the selected component turn ink.
- **Flow stepper (Atlas):** flow tabs, a step count, a step title in condensed caps, previous/next/reset controls and 3px pips. One control drives both the SVG drawing and the 3D model.

### "N More" Disclosure (Paper)
Experience entries show two points and fold the rest into a `details` toggle reading "N more" in citation blue at caption size, led by the authored chevron that turns on open (160ms).

### Evidence Table (Paper)
The table has a caption on top ("Table n.") and uses booktabs-style rules (1.5px ink above and below, a strong rule under the header, hairline rows). Values are right-aligned in mono. The evidence kind is set in mono, except target and self-reported, which are set in italic serif at ink 3.

### Citation Marker (signature, Paper)
A bracketed number in mono. Hovering it or focusing inside it opens an inline reference preview in a square, page-coloured box with a strong-rule border (150ms pop). The preview shows the reference title in serif and the URL in mono, and its touch target is at least 1.6×1.5rem. On narrow screens it opens to the left.

### Colophon and Editions (Paper)
A short closing paragraph under a hairline names the typefaces, the evidence promise and the other editions as plain links: Repository City (`?template=0`), Editorial (`2`), Terminal (`3`) and Blueprint (`atlas`) (`templates/paper/editions.tsx`). It ends with an ∎ end mark in ink 3. Two quiet doors exist: the Konami code opens the Terminal, and clicking the end mark five times opens a random edition. Neither is advertised in the interface.

### Drawing Register and Title Block (Atlas)
The register is the table of contents and also the navigation. Each row shows the sheet number in mono, the title in condensed caps and a right-aligned note, grouped into tiers ("System drawings" and "Record sheets"). On hover a row gets a 5% wash and its title gets a 2px inset underline. The title block uses a label/value grid (6.5rem label column) under the name in display caps, with Role, School and Status fields, a row of actions and a three-cell foot, every cell divided by hairlines.

### Interactive Diagram (signature, both worlds)
Both worlds render the SVG layout from `shared/content/diagram/`. Selecting a component (clicking the box or the matching row in the parts list) lights its connections in the accent and dims everything else. It also shows the component's description, and numbered connection markers tie each edge to the connections list. In the Atlas, edges draw in over a faint construction underlay (1100ms `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), and arrowheads and dimensions fade in after the line. The Paper does not draw lines in; its motion is the step walkthrough and the pulse. With reduced motion, everything appears drawn and still.

### Dimension Callouts and Kind Tags (Atlas)
A measured value is set large and centred above a dimension line with arrow tips, with its label and its source below. The kind tag is a small square-bordered label. Measured uses a full-strength border, target and projected use a dashed border, and other kinds use hairline 3.

### Theme Controls
- **Paper:** a footer segment (Auto, Light, Dark). The pressed option shows a strong-rule border.
- **Atlas:** a blueprint/whiteprint toggle in the running bar, built as a pressed-state button.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** scope every rule to `html.paper` or `html.atlas` and use that world's custom properties. A new surface in either world adds no global CSS.
- **Do** pair every metric with its kind and its source. Use the italic kind (Paper) or the dashed tag (Atlas) for target, projected and self-reported values.
- **Do** keep the accent for selected, lit, playing, focused and primary states only. In the Paper that is citation blue; in the Atlas it is revision orange.
- **Do** separate regions with 1px rules at the right step: frame, then division, then row.
- **Do** keep diagrams as SVG that reads completely without motion, and give every animated walkthrough a Pause, jumpable steps and a text equivalent.
- **Do** gate any 3D behind an explicit opt-in, load it lazily in its own chunk, protect it with `shared/three/guards.tsx`, and draw it in the world's own scene palette from `shared/three/scenes/kit.tsx`.
- **Do** let one state drive the diagram, the 3D model and the text together.
- **Do** keep touch targets at least 2.75rem for list rows, 2.5rem for Atlas buttons and 2.25rem for Paper player and view controls.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. Both worlds remove transitions and animations under it, and the Paper's walkthroughs do not autoplay.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add rounded corners, cards or drop shadows for elevation in either world.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue or a hard-coded colour outside the token set.
- **Don't** set prose, headings or buttons in a monospace face.
- **Don't** put a project ahead of the person on the Paper home page, or expand a work entry into a full case study there; depth belongs on `?project=<slug>`.
- **Don't** use the hero-plus-cards layout or a cream-and-terracotta editorial palette (Paper), or neon glows and particle fields (Atlas).
- **Don't** apply these tokens to the legacy editions (templates 0–3), and don't borrow their Tailwind, shadcn or font tokens here.
- **Don't** load fonts from a CDN. Add any new face to `public/fonts/` with its licence noted in the README.
