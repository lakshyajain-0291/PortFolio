# Engineering Atlas (?template=atlas)

Scope: whole surface, templates/atlas. Visitor mode: Experience (the drawings lead), with a title block that answers the shortlist question at once.
Audience and job: recruiters shortlisting; engineers reading architecture. Proof: confirmed ArchitectureSpec drawings, measured metrics as dimension callouts, references.
Constraints: SVG diagrams readable without animation; 3D only as opt-in, per-system, lazy, after the owner confirms each spec; self-hosted Archivo + Martian Mono.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an engineering drawing set. Each system is a sheet with an accurate drawing, and the measured numbers sit on it as dimensions. It refuses neon dark-tech, glowing particles, and the hero-plus-cards layout.

OWN-WORLD: A deep Prussian-blue drawing ground with white linework at true hairline weight and a faint construction grid. One safety-orange revision mark is used only for what changed or matters most. A whiteprint variant uses a cool white ground with blue lines. Condensed Archivo caps set the title block and drawing labels, and normal-width Archivo sets the prose. Martian Mono is used only for coordinates, sheet numbers and dimensions. Sheet frames carry zone ticks.

STORY: The visitor reads the title block (name, role, availability and resume) and flips through the sheets using the drawing register. On each system drawing they select a component to read what it does, and on systems that have one they open the 3D model to step through a flow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The cover sheet sits inside a frame with zone ticks. A large drawing register (the sheet list, which is also the navigation) sits to the left. The title block sits at the lower right on desktop and first on mobile. It holds the name in condensed caps, the role, the availability, and the Resume and Email actions, with Resume primary. The libr sheet edge begins below the fold. Signature interaction: selecting a component lights its connections and shows its description, and the same selection drives the text list.

FORM: Owner-pinned Engineering Atlas direction (plan approved 2026-10-13); it supersedes concept roll 70123c47.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
